FIFA Reviews Proposal for Increased Funding to National Football Associations

FIFA is reviewing proposals that could significantly increase financial distributions to national football associations. FIFA President Gianni Infantino has ordered an assessment of the proposal following calls from football confederations for increased funding. The matter is expected to be discussed by the FIFA Council in October. The proposal could become an important topic in international football as FIFA considers how its financial reserves are distributed among national associations.

United States Retain Presidents Cup After Dramatic Victory

The United States retained the Presidents Cup after defeating the International Team 17–13 near Chicago. The Americans entered the final day facing a challenge from the International side but produced a strong comeback to secure the required points. Jacob Bridgeman delivered the decisive point against Australia’s Min Woo Lee, confirming the United States’ victory.

2026–27 NHL Season Begins With Stanley Cup Contenders in Focus

The new 2026–27 NHL season is getting underway, with teams across the league beginning their campaigns for the Stanley Cup. The Colorado Avalanche and defending champion Carolina Hurricanes are among the teams attracting attention ahead of the new season. Several other teams have also strengthened their rosters during the offseason, setting up another competitive NHL campaign.

Virat Kohli Reaches 15,000 ODI Runs in Record Time

3

India’s Virat Kohli reached the remarkable milestone of 15,000 runs in One Day International cricket during India’s series-opening victory over the West Indies. Kohli scored an unbeaten 139 runs from 88 balls, helping India secure an eight-wicket victory. His achievement also saw him break Sachin Tendulkar’s previous record for reaching 15,000 ODI runs in the fewest innings.

Bangladesh Advances to Asian Games Cricket Semi-Finals After Rain Abandons Quarter-Final

Persistent rain forced the abandonment of Bangladesh’s men’s cricket quarter-final against Malaysia at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games. No ball was bowled during the match, and Bangladesh advanced to the semi-finals as the higher-ranked team. Bangladesh will face Pakistan in the semi-final. However, Bangladesh will be without captain Najmul Hossain Shanto, who has been ruled out of the tournament due to illness.
